What is the commission paid on?

A percentage of the earned fees on a funded business loan.

How long do the Cyber Monday rates last?

Starter pays 35% on every deal for 90 days from sign-up. Premier Monthly pays 50% for the first 3 months, and Premier Yearly pays 50% for the first 6 months. Your window starts the day you sign up. Sign up by December 7 at 11:59 PM CT.

What happens after my window?

Deals pay at your current volume tier: normally 20% to 35% for Starter and 35% to 50% for Premier. The more you fund, the higher you go.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrade to Premier by December 7 to start at 50%.

Which Premier plan should I choose?

Monthly is the way to try Premier first: the first 30 days are free, then it's $499 per month. Yearly is one payment of $3,995 for partners ready to commit for the year, with a longer 6-month window at 50%.

What kind of deals qualify?

Established businesses that generate revenue and have a real funding need. Consumer lending doesn't qualify.

What's included with Premier?

Compliance and website review, funnel builds on request, the full partner toolkit of funding, growth and ad offers to sell, and priority support from the GrowthLock partner team.

Can I get 15% off an ad campaign?
